- **Fee data**: all course-specific fee amounts are currently marked unverified in the college JSON. No fees or additional admission charges have been estimated or invented. Every course's fee fields point to that institution's official site and remain unverified until an applicable current official schedule is added.
- **Campus directions**: the pink route is a suggested line drawn over the supplied HCST campus schematic using landmark positions traced from the plan. It is not a surveyed route, verified road, scale, GPS alignment, distance or time estimate. Confirm routes on site with campus staff.

- **Official fee sources checked 2026-10-07:** [HCST fee structure](https://hcst.edu.in/admission-process/fees-structure/) publishes tuition and selected additional charges; the billing period for the retrieved tuition figure is not specified there, so the app says to confirm it. [GLA Mathura 2026-27 fee structure](https://www.gla.ac.in/admissions/fee-structure) publishes academic fees by year. - **Expanded admission picker:** `data/admission-colleges.json` combines the 165 distinct college names and 636 branch rows in the supplied UPTAC reference with the Mathura profiles (170 unique profiles after deduplication). It is not a global catalog. Every branch appears in the dashboard with its fee status; only source-backed values are marked verified.
