How we calculated

Clear numbers.
No guessed cut-offs.

  1. Normalize: each subject is converted to a percentage if your report card uses marks out of something other than 100.
  2. Average: Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics percentages are averaged equally.
  3. Match: the PCM average is matched to published scholarship bands. The estimator does not infer eligibility from admission cut-offs.
  4. Confirm: scholarship seats, annual policy changes, board interpretation and continuation conditions are decided by each college.
